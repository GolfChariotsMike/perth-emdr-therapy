What is EMDR?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) is an evidence-based therapy that helps people work through distressing memories and experiences. While you briefly bring a memory to mind, your therapist guides you through bilateral stimulation, such as eye movements or tapping. This helps the brain reprocess the memory so that it feels less intense and less distressing.

EMDR is widely used to treat trauma and PTSD, and it can also help with anxiety and other difficulties linked to past experiences.
