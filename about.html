Perth EMDR Therapy

Perth EMDR Therapy was founded with a vision to create a warm, welcoming, and professional space for clients to begin their healing journey. I am currently building a team of like-minded therapists and Psychologists who share the same therapeutic values and believe that the therapeutic alliance is the foundation for effective treatment and meaningful change.

Perth EMDR Therapy is actively recruiting qualified Psychologists and EMDR therapists who align with our values of compassion, professionalism, and client-centred practice.
